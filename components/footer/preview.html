Decisions I made where the spec was silent

  • Absolute coordinates → ratios. Every size is --canvas × ratio, where --canvas is min(100vw, 1920px). The fixed y: values become a two-row grid, so the columns hold at any width instead of breaking off the 1920 canvas.
  • Column x-positions. Left starts at 71, right at ~1089 with a ~792-wide track — reproduced with justify-content: space-between and proportional tracks (0.33 / 0.40). The empty middle is where the watermark shows. Pixel-exact positions weren't pinned; say so if the gap matters.
  • Vertical alignment. Contact details (y 533) and nav links (y 536) top-align on grid row 2; LinkedIn (y 852) and legal links (y 857) are margin-top: auto pinned to the footer bottom — so they stay aligned as content reflows.
  • Nav line_separator is a border-top on each link (grey #696969, 1px), spanning the ~792 column. Legal links are right-aligned to that same edge, per "right aligned with line separator".
  • Font weights. "Mittel" → font-weight: 500, "Regular" → 400. Apfel Grotezk falls back to Helvetica until the licensed webfont is added (same note as the hero).
  • Links made real. Phone/fax are tel: links, LinkedIn opens in a new tab with rel="noopener", legal links point at /privacy-policy / /cookie-policy. Spec gave labels only — wire the hrefs to real slugs.
  • Spec typos normalised in the rendered copy: "Contattti" → "Contatti". LinkedIn key is linkdin in the JSON; kept the class as linkedin.
  • send.svg is in the folder but not referenced by footer.json, so it isn't used here. It looks like the contact-form submit icon — left out until the spec calls for it.
  • Paragraph colour wasn't given; rendered white on black. Details are explicitly white, P.IVA and legal are grey #696969.
